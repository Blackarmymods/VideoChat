💡 App Ko Kaise Use Karein?

Step 1: Network Connect Karein 📶

Ek phone me **Wi-Fi Hotspot** chalu karein aur doosre phone ko us hotspot Wi-Fi se connect karein. *(Internet Data ki zarurat NAHI hai)*.

Step 2: App Open Rakhein 🌐

Dono phones ke browser me is website page ko open rakhein. Web page open hote hi nearby search me devices dikhne lagenge.

Step 3: Call Connect Karein 📞

  • **Option A (Best):** **"Nearby Active Devices"** list me doosre phone ka naam dikhega, uske aage **"Call"** button par tap karein.
  • **Option B:** Agar nearby list me device na dikhe, toh **QR Code Scan** karke ya ID enter karke call karein.

⚠️ Important Hints / Permissions

  • Pehli baar call karne par Camera aur Microphone permission **Allow** karein.
  • Jab tak dono phone par ye website open rahegi, tabhi tak nearby list me dikhenge. Website band karte hi device list se hat jayega.
